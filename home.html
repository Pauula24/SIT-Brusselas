WHAT IS SIT!?

SIT! is a festival about comfort, design and the simple act of sitting. A place to discover new ideas, meet people, try different spaces and experience design in a more relaxed way.

Talks, workshops, furniture, music and unexpected ways of sitting — all in one place.

A MOMENT TO SLOW DOWN AND EXPERIENCE DESIGN.

02

SIT / REST / PLAY / MEET

SIT / REST / PLAY / MEET — EXPERIENCE DESIGN DIFFERENTLY

SIT! is not just about sitting. It is about what happens when we slow down.

Sit and discover. Rest and take your time. Play with new ideas. Meet people and share the experience.

From talks and workshops to furniture, music and unexpected spaces, every moment invites you to experience design differently.
